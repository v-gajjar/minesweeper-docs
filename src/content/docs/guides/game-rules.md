### How to Play

Minesweeper begins with all cells hidden. Once you make your **first move**, a set number of mines are randomly placed on the board — but never on that first cell.

Your objective is simple: **reveal every safe cell** without triggering a mine 💣

---

#### Game Basics
- **Left-click** a cell to reveal it.  
  - If it’s a mine → game over!  
- **Right-click** a cell to place or remove a **flag** 🚩 marking a suspected mine.  
- Each revealed number shows **how many mines** are hidden in the 8 surrounding cells. Use logic to uncover safe spots.

---

#### When the Game Ends
- **Correct flags** remain visible.  
- **Incorrect flags** are marked with a cross.  
- All remaining hidden mines are revealed.

---

#### Fair Start
Mines are **not placed** until your first click, ensuring your opening move is always safe.
